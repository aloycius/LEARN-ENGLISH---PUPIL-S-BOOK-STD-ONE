Introduction

This Learn English textbook has been prepared based on the 2023 Primary Education Syllabus, Standard I–II, prepared by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. It is a revised edition of Reading Standard One Pupil’s Book One that was published in 2019 in accordance with the 2016 Basic Education Syllabus for Standard I issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology.

The book aims to enable the child to develop competences in listening. Or observing, speaking. Or signing and reading by performing different learning activities through the following: imitating different sounds. Or letters, relating words to familiar objects in the environment, following simple instructions given at school and home, naming parts of the body orally. Or signing, pronouncing English letter sounds. Or finger spell letter, recognising English sounds in various spoken words, identifying words with the same initial sounds and words with the same final sounds, combining letter sounds to form words, associating letter names to sounds, recognising numbers and talking. Or signing about times of the day, introducing oneself and others, using common greetings and polite expressions, and saying. Or signing goodbye to others.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz.
