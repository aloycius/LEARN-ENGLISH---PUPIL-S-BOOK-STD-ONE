Polite expressions
We say “please” when we need something.
We say “sorry” when we are wrong.
We say “thank you” when we receive something.
We say “welcome” when someone says “thank you.”
We say “excuse me” when we need to get attention.
Don’t forget to use these polite words.
What you say will sound kind when heard.
